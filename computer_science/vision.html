Vision

To empower students with cutting-edge technologies in Computer Science and Information Technology.

To train students as entrepreneurs in Computer Science and IT to address the needs of society.

About the Vision

The Department of Computer Science and Information Technology aims to provide students with a strong foundation in computer science, information technology and modern technological developments.

The department focuses on developing technical knowledge, practical skills, creativity and innovative thinking among students. Students are encouraged to explore emerging technologies and apply their knowledge to real-world problems.

The department also aims to encourage students to become entrepreneurs by developing their problem-solving abilities, leadership skills and confidence to create innovative solutions that address the needs of society.

Through practical learning, projects, activities and exposure to the latest tools and technologies, the department prepares students for higher education, professional careers and future opportunities in the field of Computer Science and Information Technology.

Our Aim

The department strives to create a learning environment where students can continuously improve their knowledge and skills, develop innovative ideas and contribute positively to society through technology.
