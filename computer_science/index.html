About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Information Technology at Yeshwant Mahavidyalaya, Nanded has been providing quality education and developing skilled professionals in the field of Computer Science and IT.

The undergraduate program was established in 1990, postgraduate program in 1992 and the Research Center was established during 2002-2003.

The department has maintained steady growth in academics, research and other activities while contributing to a vibrant and forward-looking academic environment.
