About Research

The Department of Computer Science and Information Technology encourages students and faculty members to develop a strong research culture and explore new ideas in the field of Computer Science and Information Technology.

The department promotes quality research by encouraging students to identify real-world problems, study emerging technologies and develop innovative solutions through projects and research activities.

Students are motivated to participate in research-oriented activities that improve their analytical thinking, problem-solving abilities and technical knowledge.

The department also provides encouragement and opportunities for students to pursue higher education and build professional careers in their chosen areas of interest.

Our Research Focus

The department aims to support research and innovation in emerging areas of Computer Science and Information Technology while helping students develop the skills required for continuous learning and professional development.
